# Uchi Mata Technique-Specific Training Content Approval Template

Version: `1.0.0`

Status: `pending_review`

Purpose: collect coach, scientific, safety, localization and media evidence
before any Uchi Mata training drill can enter production. This template is not
approved training content and contains no exercise prescription.

## Approval Rules

- Every field remains `pending_review` until a named reviewer approves its
  evidence.
- English and Persian content must both be complete before bilingual release.
- Sets, repetitions, duration, rest, frequency and resistance level require an
  approved source and reviewer; they must not be inferred or generated.
- Media requires ownership/license evidence and approval for the intended use.
- A visual concept must be specific to its experience and supported by the
  structured fields. A generic repeated animation is not sufficient.
- Missing safety, source, media, prescription or selected-language evidence
  keeps the drill unavailable.
