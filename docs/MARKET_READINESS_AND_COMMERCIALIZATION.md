# JUDO MIND — MARKET READINESS AND COMMERCIALIZATION ROADMAP

Status: ACTIVE
Effective date: 2026-09-02
Portfolio priority: 2
Primary market: International

---

## 1. Product positioning

Judo Mind must not be positioned as a large collection of unrelated modules.

The initial commercial core is:

1. Learn Judo
2. Video Analysis / Movement Intelligence
3. Athlete Profile + Progress
4. Coach / Club Platform
5. Sensei Bot
6. Training Center integration

These capabilities create the initial reason to use and pay for the product.

---

## 2. Commercial Core

### A. Learn Judo

This is the knowledge identity of Judo Mind.

Technique content should progressively support:

- structured technique library;
- steps;
- key points;
- common mistakes;
- safety;
- combinations;
- counters;
- media;
- quizzes/learning interactions;
- multilingual content where approved.

It must offer structured coaching value beyond an ordinary video collection.

---

### B. Video Analysis

Video analysis is a potential primary differentiator.

The product must clearly separate:

- measured/raw information;
- engineering metrics;
- scientifically reviewed interpretation;
- unapproved hypotheses.

No unapproved biomechanics or scientific interpretation may be marketed as fact.

---

### C. Athlete Profile + Progress

These form one retention system.

The product should remember and organize the athlete's development history
rather than behave as a stateless content application.

Target value:

- identity;
- history;
- training progress;
- assessments;
- technique development;
- coach-linked progress where applicable.

---

### D. Coach / Club Platform

Club management is commercially important but MUST NOT become an unlimited
ERP project before first launch.

Initial Coach/Club scope:

- coaches;
- athletes;
- groups;
- sessions/training;
- attendance where appropriate;
- progress tracking;
- coach-to-athlete assignments;
- essential club-level organization.

Potential post-launch expansion:

- advanced membership administration;
- payments;
- accounting;
- staff management;
- sophisticated competition administration;
- additional enterprise functions.

---

### E. Sensei Bot

Sensei Bot must derive value from Judo Mind's structured domain knowledge.

It should not be positioned merely as a generic chatbot.

A meaningful Sensei Bot should understand Judo-specific structures,
techniques, learning context and approved knowledge boundaries.

---

### F. Training Center

Training Center supports retention and coaching workflow.

It may progressively connect:

- drills;
- sessions;
- conditioning;
- assignments;
- plans;
- athlete history.

It is important, but not every future Training Center capability is required
before initial commercial entry.

---

## 3. Sensei Bet

Sensei Bet remains a concept requiring Challenger review before commercial launch.

Required review areas:

- exact user value;
- scientific validity;
- prediction limitations;
- legal implications;
- gambling-related interpretation of the name "Bet";
- international product positioning.

The capability is not a launch blocker until this review is closed.

No gambling product is implied by project scope unless explicitly and legally
decided in the future.

---

## 4. Store and sponsor monetization

The Store remains strategically useful because it may support:

- sponsor placement;
- brand partnerships;
- affiliate commerce;
- judo equipment discovery;
- additional revenue streams.

However, a complex marketplace is NOT required for the first commercial release.

Initial commercial implementation may use a limited partner/sponsor surface.

Do not populate the Store with fake commercial relationships.

---

## 5. DEMO / CLOSED-BETA gate

The first strong external version must demonstrate:

- polished onboarding;
- strong Learn Judo experience;
- working Athlete Profile;
- meaningful Progress history;
- credible Video Analysis slice;
- useful Sensei Bot;
- Coach/Club Lite;
- integrated Training Center where needed for the core journey;
- stable English experience;
- professional international-grade UI;
- privacy and security basics;
- clear subscription/product positioning;
- reliable Demo data and Demo account.

The product does not need all historical 17 modules complete.

---

## 6. INITIAL COMMERCIAL LAUNCH gate

Before asking real international users to pay:

- payment/subscription path must be operational where legally/technically possible;
- premium value must be obvious;
- onboarding must be short and clear;
- critical crashes must be resolved;
- analytics/feedback collection should exist;
- privacy terms and product boundaries must be clear;
- support/contact path must exist;
- scientific claims must remain governed;
- users must understand free vs paid value.

---

## 7. Beta validation

Before broad promotion, target approximately 20–30 representative users.

Do not measure success mainly by:
"Do you like the app?"

Measure:

- repeated use;
- feature return rate;
- drop-off points;
- coach sharing/referral behavior;
- use of Video Analysis;
- use of Learn Judo;
- athlete progress retention;
- willingness to pay;
- actual payment when commercial validation begins.

First strong commercial evidence:

10 REAL PAYING INTERNATIONAL USERS

After that:

10 -> 50 -> 100 -> 500 paying users

---

## 8. Module expansion sequence

### LAUNCH CORE
- Learn Judo
- Video Analysis
- Athlete Profile
- Athlete Progress
- Sensei Bot
- Coach / Club Lite
- Training Center core

### RETENTION EXPANSION
- deeper Training Center
- Monitoring
- Challenges / Competitions
- richer coach tools
- richer athlete development workflows

### REVENUE EXPANSION
- subscription tiers
- Coach plans
- Club plans
- Federation licensing
- sponsor/partner Store
- commercial partnerships

### CONTENT / COMMUNITY EXPANSION
- Champions Hub
- Online Courses
- Coach Forum
- News
- Country History
- other non-core modules

These modules are preserved in product vision.
They are not all launch blockers.

---

## 9. Coming Soon policy

Do not make the main navigation look unfinished.

Rules:

- usable modules are primary;
- strategically important future capability may be teased;
- avoid many disabled cards;
- future roadmap should usually live in What's Next / Product Roadmap;
- commercial presentation may show future modules;
- maximum visible future teasers should remain small.

## 10. Commercial milestones and target dates

Dates are target gates, not permission to release defective work.

- classify all existing 17 modules as Launch Core, Retention, Revenue or Content/Community;
- identify missing Launch-Core requirements;
- prevent scope growth.

PHASE JM-C2 — Launch Core Completion

- Athlete Profile/Progress;
- Video Analysis usable core;

- Training Center core.

### 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-31
PHASE JM-C3 — Product Hardening
Target:
- international UI quality;
- English-first polish;
- reliability;

- Sensei Bet Challenger decision.

Target CLOSED-BETA READY:
2026-10-31

### 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-20
PHASE JM-C4 — Closed Beta
Target:
- approximately 20–30 real representative users;
- collect behavioral and qualitative evidence;
- identify willingness-to-pay;
- fix launch blockers.

### 2026-11-21 to 2026-12-15
PHASE JM-C5 — First Commercial Validation
Target:
- launch controlled paid offering;
- reach first 10 real paying international users;
- obtain real price objections and retention evidence.

Target:
FIRST 10 PAYING USERS by 2026-12-15 if gates are satisfied.

### AFTER FIRST PAYMENT EVIDENCE
PHASE JM-C6 — Expansion
Prioritize expansion according to actual user behavior and revenue evidence,
not the historical module list alone.

---

## 11. Commercial positioning principle

Judo Mind must answer in one sentence:

WHY SHOULD A JUDOKA OR COACH PAY FOR THIS INSTEAD OF USING FREE VIDEO,
GENERIC FITNESS APPS OR A GENERAL AI CHATBOT?

Until that answer is supported by the actual product,
additional low-value modules must not distract the roadmap.
