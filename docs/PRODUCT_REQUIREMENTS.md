# 1. Product Identity


## Product Name

Judo Mind Super App


## Product Type

Global Digital Judo Ecosystem


## Vision

Train Your Body, Master Your Mind.


## Mission

Create the most complete digital platform for Judo athletes,
coaches, clubs and federations by combining:

- Judo knowledge
- Sports science
- Artificial intelligence
- Athlete development
- Coaching tools
- Federation services


---

# 2. Product Goals


Judo Mind aims to:


- Digitize Judo knowledge.
- Improve athlete development.
- Support coaches with intelligent tools.
- Connect Judo communities globally.
- Provide federation-level digital services.
- Create a professional Judo learning ecosystem.

# Module 2

## Techniques


Purpose:

Create the world's most complete digital Judo technique database.


Requirements:


Each technique must include:


Identity:

- English name
- Japanese name
- Persian translation
- Classification
- Difficulty
- Belt level


Technical Content:

- Description
- Kuzushi
- Tsukuri
- Kake
- Execution steps
- Key points
- Common mistakes


Scientific Content:

- Biomechanics
- Movement analysis
- Force direction
- Muscle activation


Training Content:

- Shadow training
- Uchikomi
- Nagekomi
- Conditioning
- Mobility


Competition Content:

- Match examples
- Tactical application
- Combination
- Counter


Learning:

- Quiz
- Progress tracking


AI:

- Recommendations
- Personalized feedback
