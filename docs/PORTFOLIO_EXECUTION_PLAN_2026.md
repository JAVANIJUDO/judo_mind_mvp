# THREE-PROJECT PORTFOLIO EXECUTION PLAN

Effective date: 2026-09-02

## Priority

1. IRAN HPS
2. Judo Mind
3. Kurash Platform — FROZEN

## Active-capacity rule

Normal strategic allocation:

- IRAN HPS: approximately 60%
- Judo Mind: approximately 30%
- commercialization / authority-building / institutional outreach: approximately 10%
- Kurash feature development: 0% while frozen

The percentages guide attention rather than enforce hourly accounting.

## Weekly operating rhythm

### Start of week
Confirm:
- active milestone;
- gate remaining;
- blockers;
- commercial objective.

### During week
Work only on:
- current milestone;
- critical defect;
- approved blocker.

### End of week
Run:
- Checker review;
- Challenger review;
- status update;
- next-week lock.

Do not switch modules simply because another idea is more interesting.

## Portfolio gates

IRAN HPS:
Demo-Ready target: 2026-10-31
Pilot-Ready target: 2026-11-20
Then: first paid institutional pilot

Judo Mind:
Closed-Beta target: 2026-10-31
Beta evidence target: 2026-11-20
First 10 paying users target: 2026-12-15

Kurash:
Frozen until portfolio reactivation trigger.

## Authority-building

Professional communication, LinkedIn, institutional networking,
presentations and sponsorship work are legitimate project work only when they
support one of the three portfolio products or the Founder's credible position
in sports management.

They must not consume the development roadmap without a defined commercial purpose.

## Hardware and infrastructure work

Hardware, network, development-machine and tooling work are legitimate when
they remove a demonstrated project bottleneck.

They are infrastructure, not separate portfolio projects.

## No fourth active platform

Do not activate a fourth product-development stream while this plan is active.
New ideas are captured in backlog.

## Quality rule

Dates create discipline.

Dates do not override:
- security;
- privacy;
- scientific validity;
- data integrity;
- critical technical correctness.

Conversely, non-critical perfectionism must not block real market validation.
