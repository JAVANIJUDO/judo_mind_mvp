## Review boundary

This pack prepares human decisions; it does not make them. Bibliographic
verification, full-text availability and extraction readiness are separate
from scientific approval. A checked software state, an article finding or a
study-group difference does not create a normative threshold, a coaching rule
or production eligibility.

- `APPROVE`
- `APPROVE_WITH_LIMITATIONS`
